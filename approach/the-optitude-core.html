The Optitude Core

Attitude · Aptitude · Fortitude

Every leadership appointment ultimately turns on three questions: does the person want to do this work, can they do it well, and will they endure when it gets difficult? We have distilled these questions into a single, defensible framework — the Optitude Core.

01

Attitude

How a leader shows up: the disposition they bring to ambiguity, authority, their people and the work itself. We look for ownership taken without being asked, appetite for hard problems, and a default orientation towards accountability rather than explanation.

02

Aptitude

The capacity to learn the things the role has not yet demanded. We assess the speed and pattern of a candidate's growth — how they absorb unfamiliar domains, convert experience into judgement, and adapt when the ground shifts beneath them.

03

Fortitude

Staying power under pressure. We examine the moments that did not go well — how a leader behaved when the decision was unpopular, the result was poor, or the cost was personal. Resilience is a track record that can be inspected.

These are not abstract values; they are observable, assessable behaviours that we evaluate with rigour in every search we run. Together they form the foundation of our judgement-first approach.
