How We Assess Leadership

Our assessment method is deliberately simple in shape and demanding in execution. Each stage is designed to convert evidence into judgement, and to ensure that the final decision rests on more than instinct or interview performance.

  1. Brief

    We define the role in behavioural and psychological terms, capturing not just responsibilities but the temperament and judgement the position will require.

  2. Map

    We build a picture of the market and the candidate landscape, identifying where the required profile is most likely to be found.

  3. Assess

    We evaluate each candidate against the Optitude Core — Attitude, Aptitude and Fortitude — using structured, evidence-based methods.

  4. Decide

    We weigh candidates side by side, stress-test the shortlist, and support the client in reaching a defensible, unhurried conclusion.

  5. Land

    We support a successful transition, anticipating the points where a strong appointment most often falters in its first year.
