Who We Help

Founder-led businesses

Founder-led businesses need leaders who can scale what the founder built — without severing the identity that made it work.

Context

The first external senior hires are the hardest. They must honour the founder's vision while professionalising a culture that has grown on instinct and proximity.

How we help

We search for leaders who have succeeded inside founder cultures before, and assess them for complementarity with the founder rather than replacement of them.

Outcome

A leadership bench that lets the founder step back from what no longer needs them — and stay where they are irreplaceable.
