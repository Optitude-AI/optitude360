About

The human science of executive leadership.

Optitude360 is a UK executive-search advisory for organisations where leadership is the difference between progress and drift. We help boards, founders and investors appoint the senior people who set the direction — and we do it with a rigour that goes beyond the CV.

What we do

We combine executive search with an approach we call Executive Search Psychology: a structured way of understanding not only what a candidate has done, but who they are under pressure, how they make decisions and how they will shape the culture around them. Every mandate is built around a single framework — Optitude Core — that we apply consistently from brief to onboarding.

Who we are

We are a senior, hands-on advisory, not a volume recruiter. Each engagement is led directly by our founder, Simon Childs, with a deliberately narrow portfolio so that every client receives genuine attention and judgement. We work across sectors and at board level, where discretion and candour matter as much as access.

Why it matters

The cost of a wrong senior appointment is rarely visible on a spreadsheet until it is too late. Our purpose is to de-risk the most consequential decisions an organisation makes: the people who will carry the strategy. By measuring Attitude, Aptitude and Fortitude — not just tenure — we give you a clearer, more defensible basis on which to choose.
