1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work and to provide information to the site owner.

2. Cookies we use

  • Essential cookies — required for the site to function, including remembering your cookie preferences. These do not require consent.
  • Analytics cookies — used only where you have given consent, to understand how the site is used. No analytics cookies are set before you accept them in the consent banner.
