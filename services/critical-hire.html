Critical Hire

Every organisation has a role that carries disproportionate weight — the one appointment that changes trajectory. Critical Hire exists for precisely that mandate.

One pivotal role

We start by defining why this hire is pivotal: the outcome it must deliver, the risks it must absorb, and the cost of getting it wrong. Search is scoped to the outcome, not the org chart.

Assessment-led

Shortlisting is driven by structured assessment from the outset. Behavioural evidence and psychometric data narrow the field before first interview, so the final stage is spent choosing between leaders — not screening them.

Speed without compromise

Urgency is respected but never permitted to erode rigour. Parallel sourcing, fixed decision gates and a partner who remains accountable keep the process fast and the standard intact.
