Interim Executive Leadership

When the gap cannot wait, an interim leader buys you the time to appoint well. Optitude360 deploys seasoned executives who are ready to lead now — and leave cleanly later.

When to use interim

Interim leadership suits the sudden vacancy, the turnaround, the transformation, and the interval between a CEO's departure and a permanent successor. It is a decision, not a compromise.

Bench quality
