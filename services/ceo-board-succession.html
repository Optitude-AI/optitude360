CEO & Board Succession

Succession is the decision a board least often rehearses — and most often regrets rushing. We build succession as a structured, evidence-based process, not an event triggered by a departure.

Succession planning

We help boards map the leadership capability they will need against the one they have, identifying gaps early and building bench depth deliberately rather than reactively. [OWNER REVIEW REQUIRED — confirm typical succession-planning horizon offered.]

Internal vs external

A contested decision is a better decision. We benchmark internal candidates against the external market on equal terms, removing the default assumptions that distort so many succession choices.

Board composition

Effective boards are assembled, not accumulated. We advise on the skills, independence and diversity of thought a board needs for the challenges ahead, and find the non-executives who supply them.
